For our capstone, a team of six of us built the website for *Unearthing Futures / Desenterrando Futuros*, an exhibition at the Harwood Museum of Art in Taos about adobe and earthen art. The exhibition runs from June 27, 2026 to February 28, 2027, and visitors have been using the site during it.

The site is in English and Spanish. It has a road trip map of adobe sites around northern New Mexico and southern Colorado, a page for each artist, information for visiting the museum, and a playlist for the drive.

## My part

I handled the hosting and set up the structure of the project. With six people working on one site, keeping the team organized was the hardest part.

I also built the analytics, because I wanted to learn how to build an analytics page.

## The analytics

Visitors are only tracked if they accept the cookie banner. After that, the site records how long people stay, which sections they spend time in, and which artist profiles, contact links and app download buttons they click. An admin page charts all of it for a chosen date range and shows roughly where visitors are on a map, based on their IP address.
